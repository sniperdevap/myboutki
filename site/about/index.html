Your own shop, no rent

Frequently asked questions

What is myboutki?

myboutki is a social media marketplace designed for sellers. Instead of renting a physical shop, sellers open a free online shop on myboutki, post their items with photos, and sell to buyers who browse, search and order easily.

Do I need to rent a shop to sell on myboutki?

No. myboutki replaces the need to rent a store. Your shop lives on myboutki, so you can sell from home and reach buyers across the Gulf without paying rent.

Is it free to sell on myboutki?

Opening a shop and posting items is free. Sellers who want the blue verified badge and top placement can choose an optional Verified plan.

How do buyers pay and receive items?

Buyers add items to their cart and tap Order on WhatsApp. The seller receives a message listing every item and arranges payment and delivery directly with the buyer.

Which countries is myboutki available in?

myboutki is launching in Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain, Qatar and Oman.

What can I sell on myboutki?

Apparel and abayas, accessories, watches, derma beauty, bath and body, eyewear, contact lenses, footwear, electronics, home and living, sports and more.

When does myboutki launch?

myboutki is coming soon on the web, iPhone and Android. Follow this page for the launch.
