7 ways to increase your shop’s sales without paying more rent

myboutki · 27 November 2026

Whether you already have a shop or sell from home, more sales rarely come from a bigger space. They come from more people seeing your products and an easier way to order. Here are seven things that work.

1. Put every product online with a price

A buyer who sees the price decides faster. List every item on myboutki with photos, size details and the price, so people can shop even when your shop is closed.

2. Reach the whole Gulf, not just your street

A shop in one area sells to one area. On myboutki, buyers across Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain, Qatar and Oman can find you, see prices in their own currency and message you.

3. Make ordering one message

Lost orders often happen in long chats. With the myboutki cart, the buyer sends one WhatsApp message with every item, quantity, price and link. You just confirm.

4. Use product codes

Every item gets a code like MB000123. Use it in your stories and branch shelves so buyers can find the exact item online, and so you can track what sells.

5. Boost only your best items

Instead of paying for a booth for a whole weekend, boost one item for a few days. Choose the goal, the Gulf countries and a daily budget from $1, and your post shows first in Home, Explore and search with a small “Sponsored” label.

6. Get verified

The blue badge tells buyers a real person is behind the shop, and verified shops appear before others in the feed and search.

7. Watch what people like

Check which items get the most likes, saves and cart adds in your insights. Restock those, and use their photos in your next boost.

The rent you don’t pay is profit

If a small shop costs KWD 500 or more a month before staff and fit-out, even keeping a few months of that rent in your pocket can fund new stock, better photos or a few targeted boosts.
