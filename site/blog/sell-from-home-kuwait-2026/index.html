How to sell from home in Kuwait in 2026: licence, photos, delivery and WhatsApp orders

myboutki · 27 November 2026

Selling from home has never been easier in Kuwait, and in 2026 it also became more official. Here is a simple path from your first product to your first order.

1. Check the licence you need

In November 2026, Kuwait’s Ministry of Commerce and Industry allowed eligible Kuwaiti citizens to run one-person freelance companies without renting a shop or office, with applications through Kuwait Business Centre and the Sahel apps. These licences cover selling your own products through exhibits, temporary markets and online platforms. Separately, the Digital Commerce Law (Decree-Law No. 10 of 2026) means online sellers need a licence.

Rules change and depend on your situation, so confirm the exact requirements with the Ministry of Commerce and Industry before you start. This article is general information, not legal advice.

2. Take photos that sell

  • Use daylight near a window and a plain wall or sheet.
  • Show the whole item, then close-ups of the fabric, stitching or details.
  • Add a photo of the item being worn or used. On myboutki you can post up to 10 photos per item.

3. Write a clear caption and price

Say what it is, the size or measurements, the material and the condition. Always show the price. myboutki writes a short headline from your caption automatically and gives every item a product code, so buyers can ask for “MB000123” instead of describing it.

4. Decide on delivery and payment

Pick one or two delivery options you trust and say them in your bio, for example “Delivery across Kuwait in 24 hours”. Tell buyers how they can pay. On myboutki, the buyer sends you one WhatsApp message with every item and the total, and you confirm delivery and payment directly.

5. Build trust

  • Add your logo, a short bio, your phone and links such as your Instagram.
  • Use highlights for “New collection”, “Feedback” and “Branches”.
  • Get the blue Verified badge: you show an ID to myboutki once, and buyers see a real, accountable shop. Verified shops also appear first.

6. Grow

Post new items regularly, share them to Instagram as ready-made images, and boost your best item to show it first in Home, Explore and search in the Gulf countries you choose.
