Booth and shop rent in Kuwait: what it really costs, and a cheaper way to sell

myboutiki · 27 November 2026

If you sell abayas, perfumes, accessories or homemade products, the first big question is usually where to sell. For many small sellers in Kuwait and the Gulf, the answer has been a shop, a mall kiosk or a booth at an exhibition. Before you sign anything, it helps to see what that space really costs.

What shops cost to rent in Kuwait

Public listings in November 2026 give a clear picture. A 16 square metre shop in Jahra was listed at KWD 500 a month. In Shuwaikh, a 30 m² shop was listed at KWD 600 and a 50 m² shop at KWD 1,000 to 1,200. On Gulf Street in Salmiya, a 28 m² shop was listed at KWD 1,300 a month.

That is before the extras. Many listings ask for a security deposit of one month’s rent plus an agency commission, so a KWD 600 shop can need around KWD 1,500 on day one, before you buy a single shelf.

Booths and exhibition spaces

Short-term booths look cheaper, but they add up. In Saudi Arabia, for example, booths at Boulevard World were advertised from SAR 1,000 for a stay. Exhibition booths usually come with design, printing, transport and staff costs, and you only sell for the days the event runs.

The hidden costs on top of rent

  • Fit-out: shelves, lighting, mirrors, signage and a counter.
  • Staff: someone has to stand in the shop every hour it is open.
  • Utilities and fees: electricity, internet and municipality fees.
  • Lock-in: leases often run a year or more, even if sales are slow.
  • Limited reach: a shop only sells to people who walk past it.
