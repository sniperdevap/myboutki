Why a platform made only for selling beats posting on social media

myboutiki · 27 November 2026

Most small shops in the Gulf started on Instagram, Snapchat or TikTok. They are great for being seen, but they were not built for buying. Buyers scroll past your post between videos, friends and ads, and your products compete with everything else on their screen.

The problem with selling on a general social app

  • Buyers are not in shopping mode. People open social apps to be entertained, not to compare abayas or watches.
  • No prices, no search. Many posts say “price in DM”. Buyers who just want a quick answer leave.
  • Orders are messy. Sizes, colours and addresses are scattered across chats and comments.
  • Reach keeps shrinking. Without paid ads, a new post may only reach a small part of your followers.

What a selling-only platform does differently

On myboutiki, everyone who opens the app is there to shop. Every post is an item with a price and a product code. Buyers can search, filter by category such as Apparel, Watches or Derma Beauty, save items to Favourites and follow shops.

  • Clear prices in KWD, SAR, AED, BHD, QAR or OMR.
  • One cart, one WhatsApp message. The buyer adds items and sends the seller a single message listing every item, price and link.
  • Shop highlights and links so buyers see your collections, branches and reviews at a glance.
  • Verified shops shown first, so buyers trust you more.
  • Boost a post to show it first in Home, Explore and search in the Gulf countries you choose.

It is not either-or

Keep your Instagram for brand stories. Use myboutiki as your shop window and checkout. You can share any item to Instagram as a ready-made post or story image, with your price and a link back to the item, so your followers can order in a few taps.

Why this matters more in 2026

Kuwait now regulates online selling. Decree-Law No. 10 of 2026 covers online sales, and online sellers must now secure a licence. A clear shop with fixed prices, product codes and order records makes running a proper business much easier than a feed of DMs.
