Delete your myboutiki account

Last updated 27 November 2026

Delete from the app or website

  1. Log in to myboutiki (app or myboutiki.com).
  2. Open your Profile and tap the menu (☰).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Delete by email

If you can't log in, email support@myboutiki.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your shops, posts and photos, highlights, followers and following, messages and voice messages, verification ID images, and your sign-in account. Payment records may be kept for up to 5 years where the law requires it.
