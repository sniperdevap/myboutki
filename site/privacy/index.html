Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

myboutiki ("we") runs the myboutiki website (myboutiki.com) and the myboutiki apps for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Account: your store name, username, email address, country, phone number, password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, never visible to us) and an optional profile photo. If you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name and email from them.
  • Your shop and posts: item photos, titles, descriptions, prices, categories, highlights, bio and links you add.
  • Messages: text, photos and voice messages you send to other shops in Inbox.
  • Verification (optional): if you apply for the verified badge, the ID card images you upload. These are only seen by our review team and are never shown publicly.
  • Payments: when you buy a boost or the verified badge, payment is processed by a licensed payment provider. We never see or store your full card details; we only keep the payment status and amount.

Information collected automatically

  • Views, likes, taps and the website that sent you (for example Google, Instagram or WhatsApp), used to show shops their statistics.
  • Basic device and browser information needed to run the service and keep it secure.

How we use information

  • To run your account, show your shop and items, and deliver messages.
  • To show public information (shop name, username, photo, items, phone number for WhatsApp and calls) to buyers. You can turn calls off in settings.
  • To process payments, prevent fraud and abuse, and provide support.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run myboutiki (hosting, sign-in by Google Firebase, payment processing), when the law requires it, or to protect our users.

Camera, photos and microphone

The app asks for the camera and photos to let you add item pictures and your profile photo, and for the microphone only when you record a voice message. You can change these permissions any time in your phone settings.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time from the app or website (Menu → Delete account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your shop, posts, photos, followers and messages from our servers.

Children

myboutiki is not intended for children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@myboutiki.com
